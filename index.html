FROLF ISLAND

Whack the seesaw. Launch the frog. Pop bubbles, bounce webs, swim streams, dodge snakes, ride elephants!

How to play
  • Aim: tap the ground to put the target there, or drag (sideways turns, up/down moves it farther/closer). In MAP view, drag the target anywhere inside the yellow circle.
  • Angle: LOW rolls farther, HIGH stops faster.
  • Power: tap SWING to start the bar, then HIT! right as it reaches the end to land on the target. Longer shots make the bar faster!
  • Flies: after your frog lands it leaps at any fly nearby, then the next one, and the next. Each one adds to your combo!
  • Spider webs lie on the ground. Land on or near one and you bounce high, and every point after doubles. Giant webs fling you twice as far.
  • Water: your frog swims in a straight line for a few points a second, straight across rivers too. A web on the bank launches it extra far.
  • Belts pay points every second you ride. Some have a web at the end!
  • Snakes pop out of holes. Land near one and it shakes you and throws you, into a web if one is close.
  • Elephants: fly into one and you bonk off it like a tree. Stop near one and it rears up: press on GO! for big points, but bad timing loses points.
  • Ice World: snow, slippery ice sheets and frozen ponds you skid across, woolly mammoths, white snakes and spinning ice floes.
  • Mountains: land on a slope and you slide down (no points for that). Land on top for the treasure bubbles.
  • Backpack 🎒: everyone starts with 👀 Fly Eyes, 🚀 Rocket Launch, 🍯 Sticky Feet and 🦘 Super Hops. Pick one before a shot. Land on a ? circle to win another.
  • Lily pads spin you round and fling you somewhere new. Whirlpools warp you to their partner.
  • Bubbles light up gold when they are on your frog's path. The higher and bigger the bubble, the more it's worth.
  • 8 shots max per hole. Everyone takes turns, one shot each. Getting in the hole early helps, but combos win games. Most points after all the holes wins.
