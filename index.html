FROLF ISLAND

Whack the seesaw. Launch the frog. Pop bubbles, bounce webs, ride belts, get tossed by elephants!

How to play
  • Aim: tap the ground to put the target there, or drag (sideways turns, up/down moves it farther/closer). In MAP view, drag the target anywhere inside the yellow circle.
  • Angle: LOW rolls farther, HIGH stops faster.
  • Power: tap SWING to start the bar, then HIT! right as it reaches the end to land on the target. Longer shots make the bar faster!
  • Flies: after your frog lands it leaps at any fly nearby, then the next one, and the next. Each one adds to your combo!
  • Points: bubbles, flies, conveyor belts, swimming, elephant tosses. Chain them for a combo!
  • Spider webs bounce your frog high and double every point for the rest of the shot.
  • Sinking it in fewer shots scores way more. Most points after all the holes wins.
